Getting started

Which devices does DoneBar support?

DoneBar is being prepared for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, and for Mac running macOS 15 or later. The iPhone app includes support for iPhone Duo. The dedicated Mac app uses the same task data through iCloud.

How do I create my first task?

Choose New task, pick a starting point and name your goal. Set a numeric target or create a checklist. You can add a picture or icon and change the units to suit the goal. The first-run guide includes editable example tasks.

How do I add progress?

The red + adds one unit. For a money goal, it adds one major currency unit; for a checklist, it completes the next unfinished step. Tapping the progress bar advances by roughly ten percent of the target, rounded to whole units. Open the task to enter an exact count or check steps individually.

Does DoneBar connect to my bank, email, health or social accounts?

No. Categories such as Finance, Business and Health & Fitness help you organize a goal. Counts are entered manually. DoneBar has no bank connection, inbox access, HealthKit integration or automatic social-media counter, and it does not provide medical or treatment advice.

How do I change the language?

Use your device’s language settings for DoneBar. The app follows the supported language selected there. Text that you enter in tasks stays as you wrote it. New purchase-screen text is currently available in English, French and Arabic, with English used elsewhere.

Sync between devices

What syncs through iCloud?

Tasks, progress, checklists and selected task pictures sync between the iPhone, iPad and Mac apps through your private iCloud database. Use the same Apple Account for iCloud on each device. Local tracking works when iCloud is unavailable, and changes can sync when a connection returns.

Reminder schedules stay on the device where you set them.

Why are my devices not matching?

Check that the devices use the same iCloud account, have an internet connection and use current DoneBar versions. Open the iCloud status on the board and choose Sync now. If the app reports an account change or removed cloud data, keep your local tasks and contact support before removing the app or its data.

Reminders

Why is a reminder missing on my other device?

Reminder times and repeat schedules are local to each device. Set a reminder separately on each device where you want an alert. Your task progress still syncs through iCloud.

Why did a reminder not appear?

Check the task’s reminder setting, notification permission, Focus and notification-summary settings. Completed or deleted tasks stop scheduling alerts. Some custom or future-start repeating reminders need DoneBar to be reopened before the date shown in the app. The app supports up to 50 reminder records per device.

Reminders also require an active trial or permanent unlock. After trial expiry, saved schedules are retained and can resume after purchase.

Trial & lifetime unlock

How does the free trial work?

In the App Store release, choose Start my free 30-day trial and confirm the free Apple transaction. The trial runs for 30 days from the original transaction date, so reinstalling or restoring the same transaction does not restart it. There is no automatic renewal or charge.

The App Store release is still in preparation. Existing beta builds may not include this purchase flow yet.

What happens when the trial ends?

You can still view, export and delete your tasks. Creating and editing tasks, recording progress, importing tasks and reminders require the permanent unlock. The app presents an unlock option, and you decide whether to buy. Your existing task content remains available.

Does one purchase cover all my devices?

Yes. The DoneBar Lifetime Unlock is a one-time purchase priced at US$1.99, or the local price Apple shows before purchase. It unlocks the iPhone, iPad and Mac versions using the same Apple Account for App Store purchases. All future DoneBar updates are included. There is no subscription.

The account for App Store purchases and the account for iCloud task sync can be different. Each feature uses its relevant Apple Account.

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure the device uses the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Purchase and trial in DoneBar, then choose Restore purchases. Allow the App Store check to finish. If access is still missing, contact support with your device, app version and the error shown. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple processes App Store payments and refund requests. See Apple’s refund instructions. Refund eligibility is determined by Apple. A refunded or revoked unlock may no longer provide paid access.

Your tasks & privacy

Can I recover a mistake?

Use Undo immediately after a change, before another change replaces it. Completed tasks can be moved back to Active. Undo is not a permanent trash folder. For a backup that you control, use Export tasks and keep the file safely.

What is included in an export?

Exports contain task content, progress, images and synchronization history needed to preserve edits. Reminder schedules and CloudKit account checkpoints are excluded. DoneBar does not password-protect export files, so keep them private.

How do I remove my information?

Delete tasks in the app and allow your connected devices to synchronize. Separately manage exports, system backups and Apple’s iCloud storage settings.

Removed checklist text can remain in synchronization history while its parent task exists. Recovery files, temporary copies or devices that have not synced can retain recent content. See the retention and deletion section for the full explanation, or contact us for help.

This guide describes the planned App Store release. Some features may differ in older beta builds. For data handling details, read the DoneBar privacy policy.
