DoneBar is a task and goal progress app provided by Music or Media Holding. This policy explains the app's current handling of your information on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Your tasks and preferences

DoneBar stores the tasks you enter on your device. These can include names, units, targets, progress, checklist text, categories, completion state, selected images and the information needed to keep edits consistent across devices. It also stores local preferences and optional reminder settings. You do not need to create a DoneBar account to track tasks locally.

iCloud synchronization

When iCloud is available to DoneBar, the app automatically synchronizes your tasks, progress and selected task images through your private Apple CloudKit database. Devices must use the same Apple Account to share this data. You can work locally when iCloud is unavailable; changes wait for synchronization.

DoneBar uses an Apple-provided account identifier and synchronization records to keep your accounts and edits separate. Your task database is not a public feed, and we do not operate a separate server receiving your task content. Apple states that another user's private CloudKit records are not visible to the developer through its developer portal. Apple's processing of iCloud information is governed by Apple's privacy policy and service terms. Your private iCloud data uses your iCloud storage allocation.

You can manage iCloud access in your Apple device settings. Turning off synchronization is not the same as deleting copies already stored on a device or in iCloud.

Pictures and notifications

If you choose a picture through the system photo picker or file picker, DoneBar makes a smaller copy for that task. This image becomes part of the task's saved, synchronized and exported content. DoneBar does not request unrestricted access to your photo library.

Reminders are optional. DoneBar requests notification permission when you save an enabled reminder. Reminder schedules remain on the device where you set them and are not included in task synchronization or exported task files. Notifications may display task text on your lock screen according to your device's notification-preview settings. You can change notification permission in system settings or disable a task's reminder.

Exports, retention and deletion

You can export tasks to a file and choose where to keep or share it. Exports include task content, selected images and task synchronization history needed to preserve edits. They do not include reminder schedules or your CloudKit account checkpoint. Export files are not password-protected by DoneBar. Protect them as you would other personal documents.

Tasks remain stored until you remove them or remove the relevant app data. Deleting a task removes it from the app's current task list; once synchronized, its content payload is cleared from the current cloud task record. Small deletion markers may remain to prevent old devices from restoring deleted tasks.

Some content can remain in a previous local recovery file, a recent Undo state, temporary synchronization files, system backups, exported copies, or another device that has not synchronized. When you remove an individual checklist step, its previous text can remain in the parent task's synchronization history. Removing a step or uninstalling the app therefore does not guarantee immediate erasure from all copies. You control separately saved exports and Apple backup/iCloud settings. Contact us for help identifying the relevant deletion controls; we cannot directly erase another user's private iCloud database for them.

Trial and purchases

DoneBar offers a free 30-day trial and a one-time permanent unlock. Apple processes these transactions through the App Store. We do not receive your card details. The app uses verified StoreKit transaction information on your device to determine the trial start and whether the permanent unlock is owned, restored or revoked. A local time preference helps prevent an ordinary clock change from restarting the trial. These purchase settings are not included in your task exports or task-sync payloads.

The trial does not turn into an automatic charge or subscription. The permanent purchase applies across iPhone, iPad and Mac using the same Apple Account for App Store purchases. Future DoneBar updates are included. After trial expiry, you can still view, export and delete your tasks; task editing and reminders require the unlock. Apple's handling of purchases, refunds and purchase history is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Advertising, tracking and support

The current app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not use the advertising identifier or track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not sell task data or use it for targeted advertising.

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send, such as a screenshot or task export. We use that information to respond to and investigate your request. Avoid sending sensitive task content unless it is needed. Support correspondence is retained for handling and following up on the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations; contact us to request its deletion where applicable.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple may provide us with beta feedback and diagnostic information under its TestFlight terms and privacy notices. Apple's App Store, iCloud, diagnostics and other system services are governed by Apple's own policies. This does not mean DoneBar automatically sends us the contents of your task list.

Children and sensitive information

DoneBar is a general-purpose productivity app and does not ask for your age, create child profiles, or provide social interaction. You decide what to put in your tasks. Avoid entering information you do not want stored on your device, synchronized to your private iCloud account, or included in your exports. The app does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.
