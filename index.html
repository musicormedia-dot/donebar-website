From an idea to a finished goal

Start small. Keep going.

01 / MAKE IT YOURS

Give your goal a target.

Choose a number or a checklist. Add a title, picture or icon, then break the work into manageable steps.

02 / MARK YOUR PROGRESS

A little more, with a tap.

Add one unit with the + button, advance by a larger step with the bar, or open the task to enter an exact amount.

03 / PICK UP ANYWHERE

Your goals go with you.

Sync tasks, progress and task pictures through your private iCloud account. Work offline and sync when a connection returns.
